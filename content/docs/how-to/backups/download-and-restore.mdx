This page covers how to get data back out of QuickStack backups. For configuration, see the individual backup guides.

## Download a scheduled backup

Volume and database backups are stored in your [S3 target](/docs/how-to/backups/s3-targets). You can download them from inside QuickStack:

1. Open **Backups** in the main menu.
2. Find the schedule and open its backups detail dialog.
3. Use the download icon on a backup row to download the archive.

You can also download a backup file from the S3 bucket directly with any S3 client.

## Download a live volume

To snapshot the current contents of a running app's volume:

1. Open the app in the **Project Canvas** and go to **Settings → Storage**.
2. In the **Volumes** card, click the download icon (**Download volume content**).
3. Confirm **Download Volume Data** and click **Download**.

The volume is packaged as a ZIP archive. This is a one-off export, not a scheduled backup. See [File Browser](/docs/how-to/storage/file-browser) for browsing a volume interactively.

## Restore a volume or database

<Callout type="warn">
**No in-UI restore for volume and database backups yet**
Volume and database backups can be downloaded, but QuickStack does not currently provide a button to restore them into a volume or database. Plan your recovery around the downloaded dump files.
</Callout>

Recovery options depend on the workload:

- **Volumes:** the backup is an archive of the volume contents. Use the built-in [File Browser](/docs/how-to/storage/file-browser) to upload the archive into the target volume and extract it there. The app is stopped while the volume is mounted, so start or redeploy it afterwards.
- **PostgreSQL databases:** the backup is a compressed `pg_dump`. Restore it with QuickStack's built-in [pgAdmin tool](/docs/how-to/databases/db-tools), which connects to the database pre-configured.
- **MariaDB / MySQL databases:** the backup is a compressed `mariadb-dump`. Import it with the built-in [phpMyAdmin or DbGate tool](/docs/how-to/databases/db-tools).
- **MongoDB databases:** the backup is a `mongodump`. Restore it with the built-in [DbGate tool](/docs/how-to/databases/db-tools).

## Restore a system backup

The QuickStack configuration database **can** be restored from the UI. A system backup captures projects, users, app definitions, and settings.

1. Go to **Settings → Platform → Storage & Backups**.
2. Click **View & Restore Backups**.
3. Under **Upload and Restore Backup**, select a `.tar.gz` or `.tgz` system backup file.
4. Confirm **Restore System Backup** by clicking **Restore Backup**.

<Callout type="error">
**Restoring replaces all configuration**
A system restore replaces the entire QuickStack configuration — projects, users, environment variables, and settings. Download a fresh system backup before restoring, and restart QuickStack afterwards as prompted. See [System Backups](/docs/how-to/backups/system-backups) for the full warning.
</Callout>

## Recovery checklist

1. Keep at least one recent [system backup](/docs/how-to/backups/system-backups) outside the cluster.
2. Configure [volume](/docs/how-to/backups/volume-backups) and [database](/docs/how-to/backups/database-backups) backup schedules for critical workloads.
3. Periodically download a backup and verify that it opens and restores on a non-production instance.
