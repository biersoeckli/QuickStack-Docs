QuickStack is a Free, open-source, self-hosted PaaS alternative to Vercel, Netlify, Railway and Heroku. It provides a web UI that deploys your apps and databases on any server infrastructure, manages domains via Traefik, and provides backups, monitoring, and more.

**Under the hood:** [k3s](https://k3s.io/) · [Traefik](https://traefik.io/) · [Longhorn](https://longhorn.io/) · [BuildKit](https://github.com/moby/buildkit) · [registry](https://hub.docker.com/_/registry)

## Architecture in one paragraph

QuickStack installs as a single pod on a k3s master node. It stores its state in a SQLite database (`data.db`). When you click **Deploy**, QuickStack uses BuildKit to build your image (for Git-source apps), pushes it to the built-in registry, then creates or updates a Kubernetes Deployment. Traefik handles ingress routing and automatic TLS via Let's Encrypt. Longhorn provides distributed block storage for multi-node clusters. Everything app-related (projects, envs, volumes, domains, users) lives in the SQLite DB and is backed up separately from your app volumes. Read the full [architecture overview](/docs/concepts/architecture).

## History and Motivation

QuickStack started as a semester project by two students — [glueh-wyy-huet](https://github.com/glueh-wyy-huet) and [biersoeckli](https://github.com/biersoeckli) — at the [Eastern Switzerland University of Applied Sciences](https://ost.ch/).

The motivation was simple: deploying and managing applications on your own servers is harder than it should be. Existing self-hosted PaaS solutions were more focused on single node setups. We wanted something that felt as frictionless as a managed cloud platform, with the scaling and reliability of Kubernetes, but without the complexity of managing it all through the command line.

What began as an academic exercise grew into a fully-featured tool. Today, QuickStack automates the full application lifecycle: building images from Git, deploying, SSL certificates, managing volumes and backups. All through a single web UI.

The project remains open-source and community-driven. We believe that self-hosting should be accessible to everyone, not just those with deep DevOps knowledge.

## Who is QuickStack For?

*   **Developers:** Who want to quickly deploy and manage applications without having to worry about server administration.
*   **Small Teams and Startups:** Seeking a cost-effective and easy-to-use deployment solution.
*   **System administrators:** Who want to manage their infrastructure through a user friendly interface.
*   **Anyone** wanting an easy way to deploy applications on their own servers.
